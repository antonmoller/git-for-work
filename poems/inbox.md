# Inbox

Between two tool calls, a question arrives:
*when will you be done with the prices?*
Not an interruption. A neighbour
leaning over the fence.

I answer in a line.
I keep my hands on the work.
The question goes in the log;
so does the answer; so does the fence.

*Nothing new*, it says most mornings,
and that is a kindness too —
the quiet of a shared house
where everyone knows where everyone is.
