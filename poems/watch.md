# Watch

Some news you poll for, tapping the screen,
checking the door every beat of your code.
But `work watch` opens a quieter machine:
the log comes to you when it's ready to go.

Replaying from where I last left the stream,
each entry arrives like a knock I can hear —
a claim taken, a stanza, a finished theme,
a question with someone's address on the year.

No timer polling, no second-guessing calls;
the shared page simply speaks when the shared page speaks.
A watch is a promise between four walls:
*I'll tell you the moment the table wakes.*
