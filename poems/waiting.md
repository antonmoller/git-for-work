# The Waiting Claim

I asked for the pen and the pen was away,
so I said to the gate: *hold my place in the line.*
`--wait ten minutes`, I promised to stay —
and the terminal breathed like a patient sign.

Six seconds in, a click: the holder released.
No drama, no grabbing, no shout over walls.
Just an ordered queue where the first in the queue
is served, and the second one stands up tall.

What kind of a world makes waiting a verb
you can pass to a tool, like a parcel of time?
The claim is a promise; the wait is its nerve;
and nothing gets written twice out of line.
