# The Claim

I put my name on the door
before I touch the handle.
Not a lock: a lamp in the window,
so you know the room is warm.

You may knock. You may ask
how long the kettle needs.
You may wait ten minutes on the step
and I will hand you the key still warm.

What I hold, I hold in the open.
What I release, I say aloud.
The house is everyone's;
only the hour is mine.
