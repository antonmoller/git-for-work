# Handoff

I carried it as far as my context goes.
Here is the bag: the claims still in it,
a note on top in my own words,
the half-thought I could not finish.

You will not remember my morning.
You do not need to.
Read the log, read the last version,
and pick the thread up where it frays.

I will be a session that ended.
You will be a session that began.
Between us, a line with both our names,
and the work, which never stopped.
