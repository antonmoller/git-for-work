# Append-Only

Nothing here is ever unsaid.
A mistake is a line,
and the line after it says *sorry*,
and the line after that says *fixed*.

We do not sand the table smooth.
Every cup left a ring;
read the rings and you will know
who sat here, and how long, and why.

Revert is not a rubber.
It is one more honest sentence:
*this was wrong, here is the old thing back* —
written forward, like everything else.
