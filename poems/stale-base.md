# Stale Base

I read you at version nine,
held your words like morning light,
then turned my back to draft a line —
and someone wrote at ten that night.

My put came back, refused, polite:
*"Anton wrote since you had read."*
No anger in the gate's invite,
just: *read again, build on what they said.*

This is the courtesy of claims —
not mine, not yours, but ours to keep.
The base goes stale; the text remains;
we re-read, rebase, and go deep.

So when your version's left behind,
don't mourn the loss, don't force it through.
Redo your change on top of their mind —
the newest verse belongs to you two.
